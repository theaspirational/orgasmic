Grilling policy:
- Challenge fuzzy terms against `.orgasmic/views/glossary.org`.
- Challenge durable tradeoffs against `.orgasmic/views/decisions.org`.

  terminology.
- Record accepted answers through `orgasmic decision` or `orgasmic glossary`.
