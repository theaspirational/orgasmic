#+title: orgasmic conversation node type
#+version: 1

* NODE-TYPE conversation
:PROPERTIES:
:COLLECTION: conversations
:ID_PREFIX: CONV-
:LABEL: Conversation
:LABEL_PLURAL: Conversations
:REQUIRED_PROPERTIES: ID PURPOSE OWNER
:STATES: open archived
:TRANSITIONS: open>archived archived>open
:CHAT_PROMPT: node-chat
:END:

A conversation is an agent chat about one node (a RELATES_TO link in its
links.org) or about the project. Its transcript is its runs, oldest first in
:RUNS:; turns never enter the ledger. PURPOSE and OWNER are immutable; RUNS,
MACHINE, MODE, PROVIDER, SERVICE_TIER, HARNESS_ARGS and CREATED_AT are
daemon-owned. SERVICE_TIER and HARNESS_ARGS are the launch inputs a resumed or
cold relaunch repeats; HARNESS_ARGS is a JSON array.
