* term_8CJ8G Team member
:PROPERTIES:
:AVOID: user account; login (as a noun)
:CANONICAL: member
:DEFINITION: A named identity on the daemon, minted by the single admin as a durable token with a role (admin, editor, or viewer). Members sign in from a browser by pasting the token at /login; their name stamps every comment they leave on artifacts.
:ID: term_8CJ8G
:RELATES_TO: term_S45HH
:END:

Roles: the one admin mints and revokes tokens and can do everything; editors generate and regenerate artifacts and comment; viewers view and comment. Member records (name, role, token hash) live host-local in $ORGASMIC_HOME/user/auth/members.org and are never committed to the repo-shared .orgasmic/ — repo files carry member names only. How a member's browser reaches the daemon (tunnel, LAN, shared host) is deployment ops, not member identity.
