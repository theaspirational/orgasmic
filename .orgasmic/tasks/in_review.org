** The headline: round 3 fixed one half and broke the other
The chain has been chasing "the gate returns no verdict". Round 3 fixed the
exit-4-on-a-calm-host half and broke the exit-0-on-a-flake half.

*Both of round 3's demonstrations dodged this by construction*, which is why it
shipped: the =--workspace= run had a REAL failure (so the earlier =REAL_COUNT=

log written by any earlier version now reclassifies as =calm= where F3

** M-3 — MEDIUM — the rate has no minimum window
=scripts/run-tests.sh:465-493=, =:636-638=. =local_wall= is =date +%s=
arithmetic — integer, +/-1s quantisation — clamped to a floor of 1, and
=host_is_degraded= gates at ANY wall. Reviewer probes:
- =wall_s=1, syspolicyd_cpu=2.0= -> rate 2.0000 -> DEGRADED, exit 4
- =wall_s=3, syspolicyd_cpu=4.5= -> rate 1.5000 -> DEGRADED, exit 4

So a 1-3s scoped run judges the whole host on 1-3 samples of a bursty quantity.
This is the short-window mirror risk the review was asked to attack, and it is
real — it simply did not fire in the two runs anyone happened to make.

*Fix:* require =wall_s >== a floor (10-30s) for the rate to gate at all; below the
floor report =host : unknown (window too short to judge)= — neither calm nor
DEGRADED. That also disposes of =wall_s=0= and the sub-second clamp, which
currently UNDER-report (a 0.3s window's delta divided by 1).
