THE REAL CAUSE, root-caused by the implementer and verified by the manager:
=resolve_authoritative_recovery_claim= required a Committed claim to be
corroborated by a link in =indexed_origins=, which is derived from =RunCatalog=
— a cache that DROPS a record on every lifecycle append by design. A replay about
a LIVE replacement therefore hit a window where the index carried no link,
quarantined a claim that had just verified itself against the replacement's own
transcript, fell through to a fresh acquire, and took =LeaseHeld= from the live
replacement. An index that CONTRADICTS a claim still quarantines; one that is
merely SILENT no longer does.

VERIFIED ON THE MERGED TREE BY THE MANAGER: *12 passed / 0 failed of 12*
consecutive runs, against 5/8 at base. Plus the new
=committed_claim_survives_an_index_blind_to_its_replacement= unit test, which is
what actually pins the mechanism — 12/12 is ~99.8% confidence against a 45%
rate, not proof.

*STILL OPEN AND WORTH A TASK:* nothing in the pipeline repeats a crash-recovery
test or scores a pass rate, so a defect at 40% ships green one round in two.
That is how this survived however long it has been there.

** Where to look
=/api/runs/<id>/recover= with =action: start_recovery_run= returns 409 from
=recovery_claim_conflict= (=api.rs:11723=), which fires when a claim exists whose
=request_id= differs, or when a Committed claim is not authoritative per
=committed_claim_is_authoritative= — and that function consults =indexed_origins=,
so an index that is not ready yet produces a conflict rather than a replay.

TASK-RMA18's daemon surface is small and entirely in =supervisor.rs=:
=AdmittedRun= replacing the lease's =String=, =worktree_identity= /
=same_worktree=, =pending_admission_in_worktree=, and the =blocking_run_for_close=
consultation.

worktree_key=) on the merged tree, rebuilt, and the test STILL FAILED. That was
the obvious candidate — a broadened equality on a conflict check can only produce
more conflicts — and it is not the cause.
