From TASK-HXSW0 (2026-07-29). Its shipped-prose guard
(crates/orgasmic-cli/tests/cli_parity.rs, 128 invocations across shipped/**)
proves every QUOTED command resolves against the real clap surface. It cannot
catch the inverse: prose asserting a real verb is ABSENT. That inverse is
measured, not hypothetical — `shipped/prompt-studio/conventions/manager-dispatch.org`
said "there is no listing command … that verb has never existed in any command
group" and `prompt-specs/manager.org` listed `manager drivers` among "commands

`orgasmic manager drivers`. The prose instructed managers to discover transport
pairs by submitting a bogus pair and reading the rejection. Both were corrected
by hand in HXSW0; nothing prevents recurrence.

=== Ask

A guard that detects negative claims about command names in shipped prose —
"does not exist", "has never existed", "there is no … command/verb" within N
tokens of an `orgasmic …` span — and FAILS when the named verb resolves against
the real clap tree. Precision matters: the corpus legitimately says "X does not
exist" about verbs that genuinely don't (that is the guard passing); tune the
extraction so real negatives survive.

=== Acceptance

- An injection that re-adds a "verb X does not exist" sentence about a verb
  that DOES resolve is caught by the test (this is the natural verify artifact).
- The guard runs in the same test binary as the existing shipped-prose check
  and shares its extraction, so the two directions cannot drift apart.
- Zero false failures on the corpus at HEAD.
