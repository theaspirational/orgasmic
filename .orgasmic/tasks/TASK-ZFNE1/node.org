** THE FIFTH AUDIT FAILURE SHAPE, and the reason this task exists separately

The stage-A doctrine round was audited four times and each audit missed one level
deeper. Recorded because the sequence is the lesson, not any single instance:

1. grepped =arch:= — missed the =@arch= prefix entirely;
2. anchored the pattern to the comment prefix — missed prose;
3. searched the whole tree — but only for the token;
4. searched for the token — when the claim was the SYNONYM "architecture";
5. searched TEXT — when the claim was *rasterized into an image*.

*No text instrument can reach step 5.* A repository audit that asks "does
anything still advertise X" has to include rendered assets — images, diagrams,
screenshots, recorded demos — or it is answering a narrower question than it
claims to.

** Why this is filed rather than fixed in the stage-A round

The graphic depicts the WHOLE framework loop, and most of what it shows is still
true. =architecture.org= still exists; architecture-as-CONTENT is stage D's to
remove, not stage A's. Regenerating a hand-illustrated hero image is also not a
mechanical edit.

Fixing it now would mean either redrawing the graphic before the excision has
finished — guaranteeing a second redraw — or pulling the project's front-page
image, which is a product-presentation decision rather than a manager cleanup.

*The cheap interim option is an operator call*: remove the embed until the
graphic is regenerated, accepting a plainer README. Recorded here rather than
taken, because it is outward-facing.
