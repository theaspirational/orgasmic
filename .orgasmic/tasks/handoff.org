=ensure_running= -> =start_via_selected_adapter= -> =daemon_service::start=,

=ORGASMIC_HOME= does not scope it. A staged home has nothing listening, so an
ordinary verb restarts the REAL daemon and every live run on the machine dies at
once. FIVE WORKERS in three outages tonight.

=orgasmic= verb against a staged home. Full evidence chain, including the three
