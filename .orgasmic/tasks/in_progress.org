** F-2 — MEDIUM — =stdout.log= promoted unconditionally into PERMANENT git history
=crates/orgasmic-core/src/paths.rs:234=; retention at
=manager-dispatch.org:319=. The arithmetic is the finding, so do not re-derive
it — it is measured, not estimated:
- Volume: =manager.dispatch_started= tx — 202 (Jun), 556 (Jul), 191 (Aug 1-6) =
  949 in ~67 days = *14.2/day*, annualizing to *5,200-6,500 generations/year*.
- Size: =last.txt= mean 4,580B (max 15,562B); =stdout.log= max *478,920B*, and
  47 of 51 measured are 0 bytes because tmux never persists pane content — the
  4 non-zero ones are stdio/ws and average *157,703B*.
- Year one: =last.txt= *24-30MB/yr* — fine, keep it. =stdout.log= at the stdio
  mean is *820MB-1.03GB/yr uncompressed*, against a repo whose ENTIRE packed
  history is *8.71MiB*. That is 10-25x the whole repo packed, ~100x raw. And
  unlike =daemon.err.log= you CANNOT rotate it: pruning git history rewrites
  every downstream clone.

The implementer's reason for including =stdout.log= is GOOD and is the strongest
argument in the change — it can FALSIFY a curated close reason, and an
unfalsifiable curated =REASON= is exactly what the parent exists to fix. So weigh
it, do not dismiss it. *Ruling: =last.txt= yes, =stdout.log= not as-is.* Pick one
and say why:
1. BOUNDED TAIL (preferred): promote the last 64KB and RECORD THE ORIGINAL BYTE
   COUNT beside it. Caps the year at ~400MB worst case and ~0 typical, keeps
   falsifiability, and makes truncation VISIBLE rather than silent.
2. Promote only on non-success (=--status aborted=, or a =blocked= finalize) —
   when the transcript is worth more than the summary.
3. At minimum, skip a 0-byte =stdout.log= entirely, whichever of the above lands.

Also: the retention policy states "keep forever" with NO number and no age-out.
The parent's own text says a policy needs a number. "Keep forever" is defensible
for =last.txt= at 24-30MB/yr — it was chosen WITHOUT the =stdout.log= arithmetic.
