=== FINDING 1 IS A MANAGER ERROR, and it is the important one

The deleted comparator accepted *FOUR* prefixes, not two
(=architecture_drift.rs:263-277= at base): =// arch:=, =# arch:=, *=// @arch=*
and *=# @arch=*. The manager's inventory grepped only =arch:= and reported it as
"MEASURED, not estimated" with a confident count of 51.

*26 =// @arch= line-1 back-edges survive in =ui/=* — =App.tsx=, =router.tsx=,
=routes.ts=, =AppShell.tsx=, =ArchitectureView.tsx=, =types.ts= and 20 more.
Nothing reads them: the surviving generic scanner (=marker.rs:169=) indexes only
=orgasmic:= payloads. So stage B left dead back-edges of exactly the kind it
exists to remove, and the task's acceptance grep FALSE-GREENED because it was
written from the same incomplete measurement.

*The manager had the evidence and did not use it.* When listing markers not on
line 1, the grep output included
=architecture_drift.rs:261: // Drift markers carry the leaf id via a `// arch:` / `// @arch` comment.=
— the comparator naming both forms, in the manager's own terminal, unfollowed.

The lesson is narrower than "measure more": *when a grep's purpose is to
enumerate what a READER accepts, derive the patterns from the reader's code, not
from the instances you happen to see.* The comparator's =strip_prefix= chain was
the authoritative list and it was four lines long.
