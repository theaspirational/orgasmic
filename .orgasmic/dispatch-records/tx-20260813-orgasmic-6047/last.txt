- Made the post-append fsync boundary convergent: grouped rewrites remain installed once the ordered tx pair has appended; the writer caches an explicit sync-uncertain outcome, retries the retained descriptor, and never appends the pair again.
- Routed sync-uncertain close failures to a structured committed/uncertain 503 instead of claiming durable success or hiding the outcome.
- Revalidated task files before close, accepted safe stale-ready-index retries, restored the task-file parse guard, and always refreshed the project projection (plus the home ledger when home-routed).
- Restored labelled lifecycle recovery for already-closed legacy bundle members whose terminal tx predates LIFECYCLE_FROM/LIFECYCLE_TO.
- Re-seeded the QGWK7 ordering regression directly as an old torn ledger, removed the close-path expect, corrected the obsolete two-write comment, and made the TCTTD red signature name the missing lifecycle transition.
