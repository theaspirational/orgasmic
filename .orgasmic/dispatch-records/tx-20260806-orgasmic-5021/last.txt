# TASK-QGWK7.1 — implementer summary

## F-2 choice
**Option 1 (bounded tail).** Promote the last 64 KB of `stdout.log`, record the
original byte count in `stdout.log.bytes` beside it, and skip 0-byte files
entirely. Keeps falsifiability (the reason for including stdout at all), makes
truncation visible, and caps year-one growth at ~400 MB worst case / ~0 typical
on tmux — against an 8.71 MiB packed repo.

TASK-RMA18 owns the reclamation redesign. Touching promote-on-reclaim inside the
gated path would either widen into RMA18 territory or ship unreachable code.
Nothing is destroyed today (artifacts stay in `tmp/`); stated deferral, not a
silent gap.
