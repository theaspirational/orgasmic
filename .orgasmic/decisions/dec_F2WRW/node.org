** Context
The impeccable design skill expects a PRODUCT.md stating who the product is for and its register (does design *serve* the product or *is* design the product). orgasmic keeps project state in native files, not vendor markdown, and the no-stray-docs policy forbids creating PRODUCT.md. A prior version of this record embedded the product baseline directly in decisions.org, but those facts are stable project identity and operating context rather than ADR body content.
** Decision
Record the product baseline in =.orgasmic/project.org= under =** Product Baseline= as the PRODUCT.md equivalent, read directly from the repo. There is no bundled =shipped/project.org= mirror: anyone working on orgasmic already has the repo's =.orgasmic/project.org=, while installed runtimes operate on other projects and never need orgasmic's own baseline, so a hand-maintained shipped copy would only drift.
** Consequences
Design work reads project.org for register, audience, brand personality, product surfaces, north star, design principles, anti-references, accessibility, and voice — the full PRODUCT.md surface. Decisions may link to that baseline but should not carry the baseline body. Visual-system specifics (tokens, type, motion, bans) live in the sibling =** Design System= section of project.org (rationale in dec_V5Y7G). The repo-root =PRODUCT.md= and =DESIGN.md= are thin pointers to project.org (=** Product Baseline= / =** Design System=), not content sources. Pairs with dec_V5Y7G (design system). impeccable's =context.mjs= can still report =NO_PRODUCT_MD= because it greps for the literal file; agents read project.org instead.
