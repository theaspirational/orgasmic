* dec_ASB1A How should orgasmic execute workers across multiple agent providers?    :runtime-drivers:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:            dec_ASB1A
:GLOSSARY_REFS: term_KH8TX term_KCGEH term_RG690

:END:
** Context
Worker execution should reuse proven HAR concepts without importing HAR product boundaries. Shelling out per provider gives quick initial runs but inconsistent event capture and lifecycle control; supporting one provider only fails orgasmic's multi-agent host goal.
** Decision
Lift HAR's typed =WorkerDriver= trait and supervisor pattern. Four drivers (claude-acp, codex-appserver, hermes, tmux-tui) share one spawn, event, lease, session, and task-state transition model.
** Consequences
Provider-specific behavior is isolated behind drivers; supervisor invariants, event capture, and worker tools are implemented once and reused across runtimes.
