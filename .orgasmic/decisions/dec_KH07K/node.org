** Context
The first release needs a boundary ambitious enough to prove orgasmic but not overloaded with later graph features. A minimal CLI-only v0.0.1 ships faster but gives insufficient product proof; a graph-first v0.0.1 is more coherent end-state but delays usable coordination.
** Decision
v0.0.1 ships core coordination — install, projects, tasks, runs, drivers, manager, skills, prompts, UI, auth, streams, tx, and sessions — while the design graph follows starting in v0.0.5. The design graph is core orgasmic but not part of the first shippable slice.
** Consequences
The first release proves coordination before the heavier graph machinery lands.
