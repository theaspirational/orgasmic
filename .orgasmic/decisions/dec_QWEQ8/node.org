* dec_QWEQ8 How should stage and run-state be shown together as a status pill?    :ui-tasks:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:            dec_QWEQ8
:DECIDED_AT:    [2026-05-23 Sat 15:18]
:SOURCE:        archive/2026-05-23_ui-redesign/grill.md
:END:
** Context
Stage plus run-state need a consistent visual across kanban cards, the task drawer header, list rows, the activity log, and the project selector. Two separate badges take ~2x horizontal space and crowd narrow kanban cards; a bicolor ACTOR:STAGE pill needs custom rendering instead of a stock shadcn Badge.
** Decision
Use one combined pill "STAGE · run.state": with no active run it shows just the uppercase stage (e.g. "IMPLEMENT"); with an active run it shows "IMPLEMENT · agent.implementing". The uppercase stage on the left carries the dominant stage color; a separator dot and lowercase run-state on the right render in muted-foreground. The same shape is used everywhere, and clicking opens the valid-transitions popover.
** Consequences
Stage and run-state read consistently in one compact shape across every surface.
