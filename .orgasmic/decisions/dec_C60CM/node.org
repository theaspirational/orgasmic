** Context
arch_EAVPP (TASK-41B0T, accepted design at source 07a5b5a) assumed that stall_timeout remained a transport-liveness clock and proposed a separate opt-in semantic-progress deadline. TASK-JK66P and TASK-VZMZE measured opposite failures of that clock: heartbeats kept a wedged worker alive, while a healthy worker running a quiet build was released after ten minutes. Implementation e7f907e3, merged a0caf362, changed the existing stall clock to read evidence of work.

** Decision
Partially supersede arch_EAVPP sections 2.1–2.4, R2, and the blanket pane-exclusion rationale in R11/section 2.2 wherever they conflict with the existing evidence-of-work stall clock. This is a reconciliation of implemented behavior, not implementation of arch_EAVPP's semantic-progress proposal.

last_driver_event_at records transport activity; last_progress_at records the latest qualifying event or positive work observation and drives stall_timeout. Heartbeat, Ready, DriverError and stderr do not reset the stall clock. PaneActivity remains a qualifying event, distinct from the early-exit predicate that asks whether the worker did any work. At an expired stall deadline, a bounded probe can credit positive work evidence; missing or unknown evidence does not grant credit. Absolute duration, idle-input semantics, terminal/finalize/cancel authority and revalidation after the probe remain separate constraints.

Pane process-tree observability is established: the historical rmux probe resolved session identity to pane PID and traversed its children. Current source supports the corresponding tmux/tmux-tui path; this does not assert that a historical rmux adapter is still present. Process-tree observation is not a normalized mapping from a yielded tool token to its owned PID, nor proof of semantic advancement. Thus the stronger StaleYield, progress-key deduplication, bounded permission-wait and checkpoint claims of EAVPP are not satisfied by this implementation.

Do not add a second clock or progress_stall_timeout_secs under this reconciliation. Those types and configuration remain unimplemented. If a measured need revives that proposal, rebase its design on the existing last_progress_at and single timeout-candidate/release path, explicitly resolving its stronger evidence predicate and compatibility before implementing it. Retain the current rule that a positive work probe rescues only a stall candidate, never an absolute ceiling.

** Original JK66P report section 6
The following is the original report's historical discussion, preserved verbatim; statements about HEAD/base and transports refer to that July report, not the current tree.

`.orgasmic/architecture.org` contains **arch_EAVPP** ("Provider-agnostic progress
detection contract", TASK-41B0T, status `accepted`, `REVIEW_VERDICT:
SHIP_WITH_FIXES_CLOSED`). It is a **design record with no implementation** —
`grep` for `progress_stall_timeout_secs`, `last_progress_at`, `ActivityClass`,
`ProgressEvidenceKey` across `crates/` returns nothing at base.

It occupies the same conceptual space and its §2 assumes something this change
alters:

- EAVPP keeps `stall_timeout` as a pure **transport-liveness** clock and adds a
  **separate, opt-in** `progress_stall_timeout_secs` for structured transports
  only. Its R2 says "same traffic, default config → today's behavior unchanged",
  and its R11 explicitly **excludes** rmux/tmux from progress enforcement.
- This change instead redefines the *existing* stall clock as an evidence clock
  for every transport, and gives the pane transports the second channel EAVPP's
  §2.2 says they cannot have ("cannot observe the yielded child PID"). The
  measured 2026-07-29 incidents are the counter-evidence: the pane's child
  process tree **is** observable from the daemon, via the mux, and separates the
  two incidents cleanly.

I did not implement EAVPP (cross-crate, four crates, checkpoint schema,
`ActivityClass` envelope — far outside this task) and I did not edit the
architecture record (not in scope, and the brief did not ask for one). **The
manager should decide whether EAVPP is superseded in part, or revised to sit on
top of what shipped.** Filed as a new-task candidate in §8.
