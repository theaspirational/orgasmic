* dec_1EEYE Must an artifact have subject nodes?
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: dec_1EEYE
:PARENT: dec_GPV4G
:END:

** Context
POST /artifacts/generate rejects an empty nodes list today ("nodes must be non-empty"). But grilling is often the step that *produces* the graph node: the operator opens the decisions page and starts a grilling artifact from a bare prompt, before any decision exists to attach it to.

** Decision
Subject nodes are optional; prompt-only generation is first-class. :SUBJECT_NODES: may be empty. Context assembly degrades gracefully — user prompt + repo-read instruction, no subject-Org/graph-neighborhood section. Per-node artifact rollups simply do not list node-less artifacts; they remain reachable from the artifact list and linkable to nodes later, once the grilled outcome lands as a decision. The grilling button on the decisions page is the first consumer (button/UI scope stays with arch_A3NSW).
