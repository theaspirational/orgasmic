* dec_JZDKT What should the notification bell's scope and layout be?    :ui-ia:

** Context
The bell surfaces "needs you" items since those moved out of the manager popover, and needs a defined scope and layout. A wide-scope tabbed bell risks becoming a second TODO list; a flat chronological list is harder to scan across parallel categories.
** Decision
Keep a tight scope — only items that block progress without the user — grouped into three sections: Questions, Interrupted Runs, and Parse Errors. Each row has a primary action (Answer / Continue / Reload) and a Dismiss ✕; a footer link "View activity →" hands off to the global Activity page (dec_E394G). On mobile the bell opens as a full-screen Sheet.
** Consequences
The bell stays scannable and action-oriented; the badge count is the sum of unread items across sections.
