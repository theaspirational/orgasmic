** Context
The current top header has only a "WS live" badge plus theme toggle. The user wanted a richer ambient surface that moves Status and Settings into the header and adds a notifications bell, without introducing an avatar/user-menu concept yet.
** Decision
The right side of the header carries four icons: a connection LED (replacing the "WS live" badge; click opens the Status page), a notification bell (click opens the grouped popover per dec_JZDKT), a theme toggle (light/dark/system), and a gear (click opens Settings). On mobile (≤768px) theme and gear collapse into a ⋯ overflow.
** Consequences
Status and Settings become ambient header affordances and the LED doubles as the Status entry point.
