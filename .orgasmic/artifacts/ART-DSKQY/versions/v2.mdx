<Section title="Final answer">
Append-only events should be authoritative when causal history, auditability, replay, offline synchronization, or conflict diagnosis matters and operations are stable, meaningful, and replayable. Use mutable state for current-value working files and projections when history is not the product and ownership is explicit (codex · gpt-5.6-luna; claude-haiku-4-5-20251001).

Use a hybrid: atomically append identified events, derive mutable projections for reads, and add snapshot plus retained tail when replay exceeds a measured budget. Append-only alone does not provide ordering, idempotency, merge policy, or concurrency safety. Snapshot for slow/risky replay, rollback points, consistent recovery, or schema migration. Compact for unacceptable storage growth, replay/index rebuild, cold start, memory pressure, merge scanning, or privacy/retention policy. No event-count threshold is universal. Preserve schema and sequence/hash boundaries, needed offline-peer causal history, validate, and make publication/truncation crash-recoverable. Snapshots do not restore external side effects or replace boot reconciliation (codex · gpt-5.6-luna; claude-haiku-4-5-20251001).
